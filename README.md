## About me

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Victoria University of Wellington. My research examines how households and labour markets adjust to major disasters and climate-related shocks, with a particular focus on evidence from administrative microdata.

My work combines applied microeconomics, longitudinal data construction, and causal inference to study economic adjustment within households and across labour markets.

## Research interests

- Disaster and climate economics
- Household and labour economics
- Administrative-data research
- Causal inference and policy evaluation
- Longitudinal household and family measurement

## Methods & tools

**Econometrics:** Difference-in-Differences · Event studies · Matching methods · Fixed-effects models

**Data:** Administrative microdata · Longitudinal panels · Household and family linkage

**Programming & research tools:** SQL · R · LaTeX · Git · GitHub

## Current research

- Household economic adjustment after major disasters
- Family labour-market responses to the Canterbury earthquakes
- Methods for constructing longitudinal households and families from administrative data
